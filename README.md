## Core Capabilities Demonstrated
* **Intrinsic & Relative Valuation:** Multi-stage FCFF modeling, 3-stage Dividend Discount Modeling (DDM), Relative Valuation, CAPM cost of equity, peer-unlevered beta, bond YTM cost of debt, and trading multiples.
* **Financial Statement Analysis:** Multi-year profitability, liquidity, working-capital efficiency, solvency, and ROIC vs. WACC value-spread diagnostics.
* **Credit & Restructuring:** Absolute Priority Rule (APR) waterfall modeling, liquidation recovery analysis, PIK deferrals, debt-for-equity swaps, and covenant structuring.
* **Data Platforms:** Refinitiv Eikon, Bloomberg, Yahoo Finance, NYU Stern datasets, and Advanced MS Excel.
